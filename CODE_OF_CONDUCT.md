# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Terchess project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Welcoming questions and beginner contributions.
- Accepting feedback graciously.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and respectful.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options available on the GitHub profile. Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles.
